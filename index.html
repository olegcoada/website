Prosuna

Visual Identity · GovTech

Identity for an open platform for digital administrative processes, built for German public administration.

Prosuna came to me from Aivot, a German software company, ahead of version 5 of its platform. The product was mature. The name wasn’t.

Known as Gover in Germany’s GovTech niche, the platform had outgrown both its generic name and its form-builder image. It now handled entire administrative processes, from digital intake to case completion.

The identity had to feel safe enough for a risk-averse municipality and modern enough to signal a way forward, without falling back on GovTech blue or a hyped startup look.

I built the system around structure, clarity and traceability, tested it directly in German, and delivered a Figma toolkit the small team could use across website, trade fair and product materials.

“Working with Oleg was a great experience. The communication was clear and collaborative throughout, and the close rhythm of exploration, feedback, and refinement allowed us to reach our decisions quickly. Despite a very tight project schedule, the process remained smooth and we completed the identity exactly when we needed it. The result is not only a brand we are happy with, but also a practical system that gives our team everything we need to bring Prosuna’s new identity to life.”

Daniel Kratz, Co-founder at Aivot and Prosuna

Lumian

Visual Identity · E-commerce

Launch identity for an AI-native Amazon agency that launched with $3M from Flybridge, Bowery Capital and Fika Ventures.

  • Launched with $3M from Flybridge, Bowery Capital and Fika Ventures
  • Live across 10+ categories at launch

Lumian was moving from experimentation to full operational responsibility for Amazon sellers. The brand needed less startup energy and more of a trusted operating partner.

I built the identity around human accountability, with AI working quietly in the background. The logo centers on a minimalist triangular prism, representing complex marketplace data becoming clear outcomes.

Light became the recurring motif across dashboards, pitch materials and digital touchpoints.

Lumian launched with $3M from Flybridge, Bowery Capital and Fika Ventures and, according to its launch announcement, was already live across 10+ categories.

Ultramassive

Visual Identity · Software

Identity built from a name and a vision, flexible enough for a software platform or a premium physical brand.

The founder had a name — strong, protected, full of potential. No product yet, no defined category. Just a vision that needed a visual foundation strong enough to guide whatever came next.

The challenge wasn’t functional. It was emotional: design something iconic and flexible enough to work for a software platform or a premium physical brand.

I drew from the name’s cosmic roots — ultramassive black holes — and built a visual system that balances timelessness with presence. The logo works equally well in a software interface or on a physical product. A set of geometric patterns derived from the mark ensures consistency across whatever Ultramassive ultimately becomes.
