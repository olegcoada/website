Endurance came to me before launch, backed by a16z speedrun, with a long-term ambition to build a stock exchange for infrastructure and other alternative assets.

Its first brand looked amateur next to the institutions it would eventually compete with. The new identity had to earn credibility with financial institutions and infrastructure developers while staying approachable for retail investors.

I explored 6+ directions before landing on one that could hold that balance and last from a focused infrastructure marketplace to a much broader exchange.
